The Forbidden
Scales

An epic dragon saga of loyalty, ancient power, and the bonds forged between unlikely companions — written by four voices, one world.

In a world where dragon bloodlines are destiny and elemental Shards define the hierarchy of power, five extraordinary dragons and their companions are about to discover that the greatest force is not the one born into you — it is the one you choose to become. The scales are tipping. The Balance is fraying. Something forbidden has been waking up for a very long time.

Dragon Records

Five dragons. Four authors. Every one of them a main character. Each written by a different member of the team, giving every dragon a wholly distinct voice, a wholly distinct history, and a wholly distinct weight to carry. Twilight belongs to all four authors together. These are not supporting characters. Every single one of them is the protagonist of their own story.

A
Agate
WindScale · Queen's Regent · Written by Elina
WindScalePescatarianPhotographic MemoryQueen's RegentBound to Saiber
Appearance
Agate is immediately striking — her scales are a layered blend of tangerine orange and deep amber-gold, the colours shifting between them as she moves like something caught between fire and honey. Her wings are a softer clementine, her underbelly faint apricot. Waves of golden scales march down her spine and forearms, catching light like armour she was born wearing. Her claws are pure gold, and her eyes are amber with bright gold flecks in the iris — eyes that look like they are lit from behind. She wears copper and bronze armbands, an amber necklace, and the long winding golden tail-band of a high-ranking advisor.
Power
During moments of intense emotion — real joy, grief, white-hot rage — Agate generates uncontrolled energy outbursts that command the wind. Early on, this power is entirely involuntary and frightening. She cannot predict it and cannot stop it. Later, as her character grows, she learns to reach for it deliberately — to turn a gust of emotion into a weapon, a shield, or a shelter for the people she loves. At her strongest, the winds around her respond to the direction of her thoughts before she even consciously commands them.
Personality Arc
She begins the story nervous and over-careful, someone who triple-checks everything and apologises out of reflex. Her cleverness is real but she hides it behind politeness, afraid of taking up too much space. Over the course of the series she becomes funny, warm, and startlingly calm under pressure — the kind of leader that others follow without being asked, and without her ever noticing it is happening. Her photographic memory is both her greatest tactical asset and her quiet burden — she never forgets anything. Not victories. Not wounds.
Role & Relationships
As the Queen's Regent, Agate holds a position of enormous political power and almost no personal freedom. She is bound to the throne's interests even when they conflict with her own. Her bond with Saiber runs deeper than she acknowledges out loud — he saved her life at her promotion ceremony, and the connection between them is one of the oldest mysteries in the records. Her relationship with Topaz is quieter: a mutual respect between two people who understand what it means to carry something heavy without showing it.
T
Topaz
WindScale · Prince · Written by Daniel
WindScalePrinceSnot WindBonded to Fang
Appearance
Topaz has the kind of presence that makes a room feel smaller. His scales are deep tangerine shot through with dark gold flecks, and his golden-brown eyes hold a sharp intelligence that evaluates everything and gives away nothing. He has a winding golden tail-band shaped as a coiled snake, a whip-thin tail, claws as sharp as obsidian, and teeth described as steel blades. A scar cuts through the thick of his tail — origin never officially recorded, though it came from a confrontation he refuses to explain. His pouch, worn at his side, has never been opened in front of anyone.
Power — The Snot Wind
Topaz can blast concentrated wind from his snout. This ability is officially, permanently, and non-negotiably named Snot Wind by Daniel, the author, under direct threat of resignation from TFS if changed. Despite the undignified name, it is genuinely devastating at close range — a focused burst of wind pressure from the snout that can knock a dragon twice his size off their feet, deflect projectiles mid-flight, and in combat, disorient an opponent's balance completely before Topaz follows with something worse.
Personality
Bitter, haughty, and protective in ways he would never voluntarily admit. Topaz has been let down before — by people he trusted, by the idea that royalty meant safety — and he has constructed an armour of arrogance around that history so effectively that most people never get close enough to see past it. The ones who do get a dragon who would walk into something catastrophic for them without hesitating. He has what the records describe as moon eyes for Agate — which he would deny, loudly, and unconvincingly.
The Bond with Fang
Topaz and Fang met when Topaz fled his home and stumbled upon the blue wolf during a pack hunt. They made eye contact and both felt something neither can properly explain — a recognition, a correspondence between their temperaments. They do not need language to communicate, though Fang can growl something approximating dragon words. Fang always knows where Topaz is. Topaz has never once asked him to leave.
T
Talon
ShardScale · Servant of Ky · Written by Raven
ShardScaleVeganNo PowerBonded to Dyno
Appearance
Small and scrawny — easily overlooked, which suits him. Light icy-blue scales, with dark blue dots scattered across the underside of his wings like a constellation pressed into his skin. Electric-blue eyes that are sharper than they first appear. A small leather pouch hangs around his neck. He has never explained what is inside it, and no one has pushed hard enough to find out. He carries a crystal dagger with a smooth rock handle, watermelon tourmaline fragments frozen into it, and the inscription: Failure is not trying, whilst winning is putting up a fight.
Power — None
In a world entirely defined by elemental power, Talon has nothing. No ability. No domain. No gift from his bloodline. He has survived entirely through wit, adaptability, and a stubbornness that refuses to let circumstances be the end of the story. Whether this makes him the weakest member of the group or the most dangerous depends entirely on who you ask. Several characters have made the mistake of assuming the former.
Personality & History
Just after hatching, Talon's parents and sister were taken from him by a palace guard. He was forced to pass tests to prove his worth before he was allowed to continue living in the palace. He saw his biological sister occasionally after — always by the Queen's side, always changed by it: snobby, self-entitled, unrecognisable. He stumbled into a friendship with Aquamarine through a collision and a mess of shattered glass, and she became the first person who felt like family. Then he found Ky. Then the others. Talon does not believe in luck. He believes in people. He is deeply shy around everyone except the people he loves — with them, he is warm, quietly funny, and completely steady.
A
Ash
MoonScale · Secret Heir · Written by Varsha
MoonScaleRoyaltyNight-PoweredBonded to StormEclipse's Cousin
Appearance
Ash is the kind of dragon who could stand completely still in a dark room and become part of it. Her scales are black and deep navy-blue in a gradient that fades seamlessly into shadow — in dim light she is almost invisible, which is often deliberate. Tiny silver stars are scattered across her wings like actual sky pressed into membrane. Her eyes are blue, with a depth to them that seems greater than it should be, as though there is more light behind them than in front. In full moonlight, she is breathtaking. In daylight, she looks like something that belongs elsewhere.
Power & Status
Ash draws power directly from the moon — which means her strength rises and falls with the lunar cycle. At a full moon, she is formidable. In direct sunlight, she is diminished, and she knows it. She is a MoonScale royal, technically in line to the throne after Princess Cyllene and Prince Nighthunter, but she is a runaway hiding what she is. She carves weapons from wood in the dark and calls them daggers. She carries citrine stones. She carved Eclipse's face from memory and embedded the jewels as eyes.
Personality & Bond with Storm
Guarded with strangers — not out of unkindness but out of earned caution. Get past that wall and you find someone who is fun, intensely driven, and frighteningly smart, with a dry humour that catches people off guard. She says nothing she does not mean. Ash and Storm share a connection that cannot be explained and has never needed to be — they communicate in feelings and images across enormous distances. Storm's hunting instinct and Ash's strategic thinking have never once conflicted.

Twilight
GalaxyScale · Twiy · Shared by all four authors
GalaxyScaleWantedDual DomainBonded to Angel
Appearance
Twilight's scales are pink and purple in a mix that shifts and catches light differently depending on the angle — stand in front of her and she looks one way, step to the side and she looks entirely different, like a gemstone that refuses to be singular. A dab of royal blue sits at the end of her tail. Her claws are rose gold. And her eyes — look at them closely and there is what appears to be a star-shaped marking in the iris, like a birthmark in the shape of a constellation. It does not move. It does not change. It has been there since she hatched.
Power
As a GalaxyScale, Twilight draws from the galaxy and stars simultaneously — light and dark in equal measure, neither one dominant. During moments of extreme emotion, she releases uncontrolled blasts of raw power and energy that are difficult to predict and harder to stop. She is one of the rarest and most potentially powerful types of dragon alive. Someone knows this. Someone has decided that this makes her a problem that needs to be resolved.
Personality & Status
Kind without conditions, adventurous to a fault, and constitutionally incapable of being anything other than completely honest even when honesty is the least convenient possible option. She is funny and generous and clumsy in ways that make everyone around her simultaneously fond of her and slightly anxious. Her recklessness is not thoughtlessness — it is a deep, genuine belief that the world is worth running toward. She calls herself Twiy and most people who love her call her that too. Currently wanted by an unknown faction for reasons not yet disclosed in the official records.
The Books

Twenty main volumes. Fifteen special stories and editions. Each book building on the last, each one a step deeper into a world that keeps revealing new rooms. No spoilers. Only promises and hooks designed to keep you up past midnight.

The Shards

The most powerful dragon bloodlines in recorded history. Each Shard commands absolute dominion over a fundamental force of the world — not as a skill, not as a learned ability, but as a birthright written into their scales. Old scholars called them Shards because they believed each bloodline was a fragment broken from something far greater. No two Shards are equivalent. No Shard is safe to underestimate.

The Great Balance

Nothing in the world of the Forbidden Scales exists without its opposite. The ancient scholars called it the Correspondence — the principle that the world maintains itself not through peace, but through opposition. Every power has a mirror. Every strength has a counter. When one Shard rises unchecked, another is drawn toward it like tide to shore.

The Wolf Pack

The Red Wolf pack — 28 members strong, led by Fang and the injured Storm. Bonded to the dragon companions through something older than language. Not pets. Not weapons. Not servants. Partners — with their own hierarchy, their own opinions, and their own reasons for being here.

Companions & Familiars

Not every bond in this series is between dragons. Some of the most important relationships are between a dragon and the smaller, stranger creature that decided — without being asked — that this particular dragon was worth staying near.

Saiber
Elina's companion · Male peregrine falcon
Grayish-blue head, brown and white wings. Loves strawberries and marshmallows specifically. Non-mythical, which in this world means he has no power beyond being a peregrine falcon — which is objectively enough.
Dyno
Raven's companion · Male hawk
A hawk with an enormous personality relative to his physical size. Loves blueberries. A narcissist, presented as a perspective. His story is narrated entirely by him.
Angel
Twilight's companion · Small bonded familiar
Quiet, watchful, and oddly protective. Angel and Twilight share the kind of bond that does not need a public explanation.

The Laws of the World
No omnipotent characters — Eclipse is one standing exception, along with the Shards, and they still have consequences.
No all-powerful magic — wishing or willing anything into existence is prohibited.
No mythicals except dragons — wolves are extraordinary but not mythical.
Body limits apply — every dragon must fit within their Shard type's physical range.
Four pages minimum per chapter — no exceptions.
Companions stay companions — they are part of the story, not the main characters.
The Current Font
The manuscript is currently typeset in Times New Roman — chosen for its clean, standard book quality. Though for some parts of the book, the font may change depending on the context.
